The Blood Feuds

Some of you have been playing the same person for fifteen years and it has not gotten kinder

Nobody plays everybody 27 times. Ten teams, thirteen regular-season games a year through 2020, fourteen since. You face nine other people. The schedule plays favorites. Over fifteen years the most any two of you have collided is 27, and that happened twice: Kevin vs CadillacKulpDeville, and pat kulp vs Natalie. The lifers mostly sit in the low twenties. Ryan has barely gotten started. senftiva left before the series could get mean. Playoffs are a different country. These are the regular-season games you actually had to live through.

The Record Book

The trophy is one number. The rest of the file is meaner.

Fifteen seasons of things nobody should be proud of, a few they should, and one 0–14 that I am going to mention until Al changes the name again.

0–14
Al · 2025 · the floor

Fourteen regular-season games. Zero wins. A fourteen-game losing streak that ate the entire year, then last place on top of last place. The same season Natalie went 14–0. The same man once won nine in a row in 2014. That is not a career. That is a horror movie with a prequel.

14–0
Natalie · 2025 · undefeated, then a silver medal

Perfect regular season. The only 14–0 in league history. Playoff bye, semifinal over Kevin, then Mike in the final, 173.7–136.0. She has also never completed a trade. Not once. Fifteen years, 107 waiver moves, zero deals. She sets it, forgets it, runs the table, and still has to look at WhoseHungry? lifting it.

2/15

Haefner, 2015 and 2016. The only repeat, and the only two years the points leader lifted it. Everyone else who led the league in scoring went home with a story. Scoring the most is not a championship. It is a participation trophy with better PR.

The Bits That Stuck

What fifteen years actually left on the floor

CadillacKulpDeville has three titles and one team name. Same name, every August, like a threat. Haefner is the only repeat in league history — Medina Sod, 2015 and 2016 — and then the rest of you spent a decade trying to catch a man who already left.

9ofalltrades stood on eight podiums before he finally took one home in 2024. Thirteen team names. The most consistent almost this league has ever produced. Mike needed eight years between rings and then did it from the same chair CadillacKulpDeville won from in 2011.

Al has zero titles and fifteen names. senftiva lasted nine seasons, never made a podium, and left. Natalie has one ring, one team name, and three men she has never been able to solve. Ryan showed up in 2020, finished last, and won the whole thing the next year.

You'll never get closer than four tenths of a point. 2018 championship · Michaeopteryx 153.76, Haefner 153.36

That is still the closest final in the file. Human Psentipede over Medina Sod by the width of a kicker. The closest regular-season game is even thinner: 2012 week 5, 9ofalltrades 115.78, Natalie 115.74. Four hundredths. Haefner has not forgotten the final. Michaeopteryx has not stopped bringing it up. I am not going to be the one who tells them to let it go.

Closing Argument

See you in September

Al has a new pun every August and an empty case. senftiva had nine years and nothing to show the room. That is the ledger.
