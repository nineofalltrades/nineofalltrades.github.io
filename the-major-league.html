The Bits That Stuck

What fifteen years actually left on the floor

Kulp has three titles and one team name. CadillacKulpDeville, every August, like a threat. Haefner is the only repeat in league history — Medina Sod, 2015 and 2016 — and then the rest of you spent a decade trying to catch a man who already left.

9ofalltrades stood on eight podiums before he finally took one home in 2024. Thirteen team names. The most consistent almost this league has ever produced. Mike needed eight years between rings and then did it from the same chair Kulp won from in 2011.

Al has zero titles and fifteen names. senftiva lasted nine seasons, never made a podium, and left. Natalie has one ring, one team name, and two men she has never been able to solve. Ryan showed up in 2020, finished last, and won the whole thing the next year.

You'll never get closer than four tenths of a point. 2018 championship · Michaeopteryx 153.76, Haefner 153.36

That is still the closest final in the file. Human Psentipede over Medina Sod by the width of a kicker. Haefner has not forgotten. Michaeopteryx has not stopped bringing it up. I am not going to be the one who tells them to let it go.

Closing Argument

See you in September

The names on the trophy are the names on the trophy. Kulp has three. Four of you have two. Four of you have one. Al has a new pun every August and an empty case. senftiva had nine years and nothing to show the room. That is the ledger. The rest is argument, and you will have it again this year, in the same group chat, about the same people.

Mike and 9ofalltrades are still tied. Natalie still has to play pat kulp. Haefner still has to look at 153.36. The commissioner still hasn't changed the team name. None of that is getting resolved in this document.

Bring a board. Bring a grudge. Try not to sit the guy who scores 53.
