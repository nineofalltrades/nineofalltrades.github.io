↓ It started in Green Bay
Thursday Night

Falcons 35, Packers 14.

Green Bay scored first. Then Atlanta scored the next 27 points, possessed the ball for 37 minutes and change and piled up 498 yards. Matt LaFleur called it “a butt-whoopin’,” and it’s hard to argue. Bijan Robinson ran 29 times for 194 yards and two scores. Drake London caught nine balls for a career-high 194. Michael Penix Jr., in his first game since tearing his ACL last November, went 18-of-25 for 256, one touchdown and one pick, and was never asked to do much more than hand it off and throw it to London.

The Packers, still without Josh Jacobs (Commissioner’s Exempt List) and Jayden Reed (neck), ran nine times for nine yards. That left Jordan Love to throw it 53 times for 312 and two touchdowns, most of it after the game was over, which is exactly the kind of football fantasy managers love and Packers fans don’t.

Seven players from this league were in it, not counting the ones sitting on benches. Five of them beat their number comfortably. Two of them lost by about a touchdown. There were no middles.

Final Word

Three flips. Three days left.

Last week one game flipped two matchups. This week one game flipped three, and it wasn’t even close.

Curtis and Mike E woke up Friday as favorites because of Packers they barely thought about. Chad and Janice woke up as underdogs for the same reason. Dao was a sixteen-point favorite on Thursday afternoon and now needs Jalen Hurts on Monday night. That’s the job.

Team KAS and Henrys Heros are the only two who can say Thursday had nothing to do with it. They’re 0.72 apart and sitting on the median line. Theirs is the game to watch.

And TJ: it’s Friday. There are kickers on waivers. It takes forty seconds. You’ve heard this before.

See you at one.
