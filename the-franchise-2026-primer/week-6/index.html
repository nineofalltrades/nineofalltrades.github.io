Thursday Night

Broncos 17, Seahawks 14 (OT).

Denver’s defense won this one. Bo Nix threw for 107 yards, which makes it Denver’s first win with fewer than 175 passing yards since 1983. The defense did everything else. It sacked Sam Darnold five times, hit him 17 times, and intercepted him twice: Kris Abrams-Draine with 1:25 left in regulation, then Ja’Quan McMillian in overtime on a throw to Rashid Shaheed. Wil Lutz ended it with a 53-yard field goal.

Denver scored its 14 points early, on a J.K. Dobbins touchdown and a 1-yard RJ Harvey plunge, to lead 14–3. Seattle came back with a Holani rushing touchdown and a two-point catch by Shaheed to make it 14–11 with 5:41 left. Shaheed’s 31-yard punt return set up Jason Myers, who tied it from 34 with three seconds on the clock. Darnold threw 51 times for 278 yards, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba caught 12 of them for 134. Denver played without Patrick Surtain II and Talanoa Hufanga.

Six of this league’s starters played. Two beat their projections by a lot. Two landed right on their number. Two missed, and one of those came with a boom attached: Mike S got Dobbins and the Seahawks defense out of the same game.
