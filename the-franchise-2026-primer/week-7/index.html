Thursday Night

Bears 30, Patriots 24.

This was Chicago’s game, then New England’s, then Chicago’s again. Luther Burden did the damage: four catches, 130 yards, a 58-yard catch-and-run that set up the first touchdown, and a screen on third-and-17 with 14 seconds left in the half that he took 24 yards to the house. Caleb Williams threw for 308 and two scores with no interceptions. D’Andre Swift ground out 22 carries and 78 yards, plus his seventh touchdown of the year. Then Cairo Santos kicked a career-long 58-yarder with 1:34 left, because why not.

The Patriots did not go quietly. Drake Maye threw for 321 and two touchdowns, ran one in from seven yards, and gave one back on an interception. Rhamondre Stevenson caught a 48-yarder. Eli Raridon caught a 30-yarder. They also took ten penalties and missed sixteen tackles, which is the only way to lose a game you were never out of. Rome Odunze finished it off with a 21-yard grab on third-and-9 and a recovered onside kick. Cole Kmet went down with a knee and corner Malik Muhammad with a toe, so Chicago won and paid for it.

Seven starters from this league were on that field. Six beat their number. The seventh was A.J. Brown, who caught three for 48 yards and went home with 6.30.

Final Word

Thursday flipped it. Sunday decides it.

Four favorites changed hands in one night, the median line is 0.60 wide, and Chad is up 24 and still behind.

If the new chalk holds, TJ goes to 10–4 and stays alone in first. That doesn’t mean much. The #1 seed has won this league three times in twelve years. First place is a favorite, not a verdict, and the guy in second is two games back.

TJ: you were favored by 2.59 before kickoff, and you’re a 1.09 underdog this morning because a running back you don’t own carried the ball 22 times. Welcome to the lineup card.

Curtis: the rematch is yours if you want it. Last December you left 22 points on the bench and lost by 2.84. Start the right people.

Chris: Burden handed you 11.69 over his projection and turned an 11-point deficit into a lead. You’re 0.04 over the median. Do not touch anything.

Eric: it’s Stroud, it’s Chris, and you’re 0.60 short of the median line. You’ve been here before. You’ve got four rings and one memory you’d like back.

Chad: Maye scored 34.24, which is 0.20 more than Lawrence gave you last week when you benched Maye. Lawrence is on bye. The court has no comment.

Mike S: Loveland dropped two passes and still beat his projection by 7.71. That’s the whole league in a sentence.

Mike E: a running back on bye is in your lineup, and so are three more on your bench. You’re the best in the building at the lineup card. Check it twice.

Rob: Caleb closed the gap with 0.31 to spare, and your receiver room reads like a medical chart. Odunze says hello.

Janice: twelve years of never busting on star money, and now you’ve spent $3 of FAAB. The market welcomes you.

Dao: the highest projection in the league, a $10 tight end on your bench, and Mike E on the other side. Don’t get cute.
