Thursday Night

Bills 41, Lions 31.

Buffalo scored on its first four possessions and punted twice all night. Josh Allen threw for 248 and three scores, ran for 69 and two more, and became the only man in NFL history with four career games of three passing and two rushing touchdowns. Detroit answered with 327 yards and four touchdowns from Jared Goff and lost by ten anyway, which is a neat trick.

Ten men from this league were in it. Six of them beat their number, one landed on it to within five hundredths, and two of them — both wide receivers, both in the worst possible lineups — put up a combined 4.30.

The Damage

Where it left everybody.

One housekeeping note before the numbers: two of these lineups have an empty slot in them, and Yahoo is scoring that slot as a zero. It won’t be a zero on Sunday. An average starting defense in this league projects 7.1 and an average kicker 7.5, so both of those teams are being undersold by roughly a touchdown. Nine-Man Football

Two of you cut a guy and moved on with your lives.

Tuesday evening, 6:44pm: TJ drops the Jaguars defense. Tuesday evening, 6:53pm: Eric drops Cameron Dicker. Nine minutes apart. Neither of them has signed a replacement, and it is now Friday. Dicker, for the record, did not stay unemployed — Dao claimed him off waivers overnight and is starting him, which means Eric spent Tuesday improving D-Unit’s kicking game.

To be fair about it: these get fixed. Both slots fill with a warm body worth seven or eight points, and the projections everybody is staring at right now are understating two teams by about that much. So here’s the honest version.

Nico’d & Dimed · no DEF

Sign an average defense and TJ goes from a 14-point underdog to a 7-point underdog in a game that started the week as a coin flip. That is the difference between a bad beat and a bye. He has until one o’clock Sunday and there are defenses on waivers right now.
The McClusterfucks · no K
116.28 → ~123.8
Same fix, less consolation. Eric closes the gap on Team KAS from 31.84 to about 24, which is still a blowout but a blowout with dignity. He is 0–2, last in the league, and currently declining free points on principle.
Key StatThe two managers fielding nine men are the two lowest projections in the league. Fix the slots and they’re the seventh and eighth. Roughly fifteen points of projection are sitting in the free agent pool costing nobody a dollar.

The Wire

Who did what.

Biggest spend of the week
$16 — Stefon Diggs
Sherlock cut Xavier Worthy and paid sixteen real dollars for Diggs, who projects 9.96 and starts at flex in Dallas. Mike E is down to $84, the smallest budget in the league, and 0–2. Big swings from a man in eighth.
Best move nobody noticed
Lions DEF, out
The Annexation of PR swapped Detroit’s defense for Seattle’s on Wednesday afternoon, about thirty hours before that unit was asked to deal with a quarterback who scored five touchdowns. Curtis is 2–0 and getting lucky on purpose.
The one that may sting
Michael Mayer, dropped
D-Unit paid $1 for Mayer in Week 1, then cut him Wednesday for Juwan Johnson, who promptly turned up on the report with an illness. Mayer is the tight end playing while Brock Bowers’ knee heals. Dao dropped Travis Kelce to sign him in the first place. It’s been a busy two weeks in there.
Name-brand casualty
George Kittle, waived
J Squad cut Kittle midweek for Rashid Shaheed and is 2–0 anyway. Janice has spent zero dollars of FAAB all season and sits second in the standings, which is either discipline or indifference and pays the same either way.
Final Word

Three days left.

One football game moved five matchups, flipped two of them, and cost nobody anything they could have prevented. Everything from here you can.

Dao was a favorite over the first-place team on Thursday afternoon and is an eleven-point underdog on Friday morning, and there was nothing he could have done about it. Rob lost a coin flip he was actually winning by fifteen hundredths of a point. Those are the breaks and they even out, allegedly.

What doesn’t even out is two of you walking into Sunday with an empty roster slot because you cut somebody on Tuesday and got distracted. There are fifteen points of free agent sitting there. It takes forty seconds.

See you at one.
