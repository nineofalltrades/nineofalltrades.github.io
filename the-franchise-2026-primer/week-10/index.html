The Franchise · 2026 Week 10 Primer · By Mohn Jadden

Sixty-Six Yards
to First Place.

The best fantasy night on Thursday went to a Giants tight end in a Giants loss. Isaiah Likely’s 24.20 turned Ken You Not Pickett from an 8.89-point underdog into a 4.84-point favorite, and the man who actually won the game, Stefon Diggs, did it from Mike E’s bench. Rob and TJ are both 13–5, and Yahoo has TJ clearing the median while Rob comes up 3.94 short of it, which would leave TJ alone in first. Blackhat has TJ losing to Chad and the tie holding. Meanwhile Team KAS is starting two Bears on a Chicago bye.

↓ It started at MetLife
The Headline

Commanders 24, Giants 21.

The lead changed hands six times. Jayden Daniels threw his first two interceptions of the season on back-to-back fourth-quarter drives, J.J. McCarthy hit Likely for a 66-yard touchdown to go up 21–17 with 8:13 left, and Washington looked finished. Then a bad Giants punt, a pass interference, and Daniels found Diggs behind the defense for 32 yards with 57 seconds left. McCarthy’s deep ball on the last drive went to Rasul Douglas. Daniels finished 23 of 33 for 249 and three touchdowns, and Cam Skattebo, back from the hamstring, scored twice.

Six of our starters played, all in two matchups, and nobody left the game hurt. Likely and Skattebo blew past their numbers, and Malik Nabers and Dominic Zvada both got buried. The only favorite that changed was in The Annexation of PR vs. Ken You Not Pickett, and the bench produced two of the night’s best lines: Daniels’ 26.16 for TJ and Diggs’ 17.30 for Mike E.

Final Word

Check your bench.

A tight end flipped a matchup, a kicker almost flipped another, and two of Thursday’s best lines were on benches.

Mike S: Loveland and Santos are both Bears, and the Bears are off. Swapping in Henry, Boswell and Pickens is worth 20.80 projected points, all three are already on your roster, and Adonai Mitchell is starting again after last week’s goose egg.

Rob: Likely bailed you out and you spent your first waiver dollars. You’re still 3.94 under the median on Yahoo, and that’s the difference between sharing first place and chasing it.

TJ: Yahoo says you’re alone in first by Sunday night and Blackhat says Chad beats you by less than a point. Tuten hasn’t practiced all week.

Mike E: no byes this week, so that’s progress. You did start Nabers over Diggs, though, and Diggs caught the game-winner.
