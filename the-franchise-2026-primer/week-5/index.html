Thursday Night

Buccaneers 24, Cowboys 16.

Tampa Bay came in 0–4 with Baker Mayfield out (dislocated thumb on his throwing hand) and Jalon Daniels, an undrafted rookie making his second start, under center. His first start was a three-interception loss. This one was 19-of-25 for 189, a touchdown and no turnovers, and he mostly just handed it to Bucky Irving. The opening drive went 15 plays and took nearly 11 minutes off the clock, capped by a 5-yard Daniels-to-Irving touchdown. Irving opened the second half with a 72-yard run that set up Emeka Egbuka’s 14-yard jet-sweep touchdown, then plunged in from the 1. Tampa ran for 241 yards. It’s their first win of the year.

Dallas was sloppy. Dak Prescott threw for 316 and a 99-yard fourth-quarter drive ended in a George Pickens touchdown, but he also threw two interceptions, his first two-pick game of the season, and the second one, off a tipped ball, ended it. KaVontae Turpin fumbled a kickoff. And CeeDee Lamb, coming off 17 catches and 189 yards, caught two balls for nine yards on a season-low 34 snaps before leaving with a quad injury. The Cowboys fall to 2–3.

Nine of this league’s starters played. Two of them blew past their number. Three landed close enough. Four came up well short, and one of those four belongs to a man who was a must-start an hour before kickoff.

The Board

Two leaders, two bad weeks.

Here’s the board that should worry the top of the standings. Janice and Chris are both 6–2. Both are underdogs. Both are projected under the Live Proj median of 123.84. If the numbers hold, the two of them go a combined 0–4 this week, and Nico’d & Dimed walks into Week 6 alone in first at 7–3, with Janice, Chris and Chad stacked behind at 6–4.

The median is splitting things a little differently than the spreads this week. Rob is an underdog but projected over the line. Chad is a favorite but projected under it. Everybody else is lined up for 2–0 or 0–2. And it’s a bye week, which means some of you are starting players you picked up yesterday. Let’s go through it.

Final Word

Bye week. Leaders beware.

Both teams at the top are underdogs, both are under the median, and both are missing players they’d normally start.

If the chalk holds, TJ is alone in first at 7–3, and Janice, Chris and Chad are tied right behind him at 6–4. This league has been wrong about favorites plenty of times already, and a coin-flip game full of questionable receivers is a good place to be wrong again.

Rob: Bucky Irving gave you 33.60. Your training room took it back.

Curtis: you streamed a defense against Bucky Irving. Bold.

See you at one. Except for the Jaguars and Eagles, who kick off at 9:30 in London. Bhayshul Tuten, Parker Washington, Trevor Lawrence, Jakobi Meyers and Cam Little, set your alarms.
