Thursday Night

Packers 27, Panthers 19.

Green Bay fumbled the opening kickoff, went three-and-out, spotted Carolina a field goal, and then scored on three of its next four possessions like none of that happened. Jordan Love completed 30 of 41 for 288 yards and three touchdowns: 12 yards to Tucker Kraft, 24 to Matthew Golden, and four to Josh Jacobs, who also punched one in from a yard out. It was 27–10 in the third quarter and the rest was paperwork.

Carolina made it interesting late. Bryce Young hit Jalen Coker for 26 yards and a touchdown with 5:37 left, the two-point try failed, and the last two Panther drives ended in a turnover on downs at midfield and a sack. Young finished 23 of 41 for 209, a touchdown and a pick, and got sacked six times. Chuba Hubbard was Carolina’s whole offense: 22 carries, 81 yards and a touchdown, plus seven catches for 38.

The scary moment was Christian Watson. He took a hit on a 53-yard catch that set up Jacobs’ first score, went to the hospital, and broke a rib. The CT scan showed no internal bleeding, he was cleared, and he came back and finished with eight catches for 113. Tough guy. He also lost a fumble.
