The Franchise · 2026 Week 8 Primer · By Mohn Jadden

First Place,
1.71 Points Wide.

Nine of this league’s starters played at Lambeau on Thursday night, and seven of them beat their number. The two that didn’t were enough to flip exactly one favorite, and of course it was the guy in first place. Njigba Please banked 36.52 points from two players and still trails by 12, because their kicker plays for Houston and Houston is on a bye. Again with the byes, Mike. Sunday comes down to a median line where 0.85 of a point keeps TJ in first and 0.86 is all Rob needs to take it from him.

↓ It started in Green Bay
The Headline

Packers 27, Panthers 19.

Green Bay fumbled the opening kickoff, gave up a field goal, and then took over anyway. Jordan Love went 30-of-41 for 288 yards and three touchdowns, to Tucker Kraft from 12, to Matthew Golden from 24, and a four-yard flip to Josh Jacobs. Jacobs also punched one in from a yard out, so it was 27–10 in the third quarter. Carolina outgained Green Bay 109 yards to 32 in the fourth quarter and Jalen Coker got a late touchdown, but Bryce Young was sacked six times and his last real chance died on an incomplete fourth-and-5 at midfield with 2:41 left.

The scary moment was Christian Watson. He got hurt on a 53-yard catch in the second quarter, left in an ambulance, got a broken rib diagnosed at the hospital (per the AP), and then came back late in the third quarter wearing extra padding. He finished with 8 catches for 113 yards, which is a better night than most healthy receivers have. The other thing to notice: Chuba Hubbard and Tetairoa McMillan both play for HoldMyCHUBBBA, one boomed and one busted, and Chad came out 2.69 ahead anyway.

The Board

Final Word

Sunday is about the median.

One favorite flipped, one kicker is on a bye, and first place comes down to 1.71 points of median.

TJ: you’re the defending champ, in first, and an underdog by 5.47 thanks to Janice’s tight end. You can lose this one and still go 11–5. Just beat the line.

Rob: you’re 0.86 under the median, with Lamar on your bench and Saquon in your flex. The fix is right there, so make it.

Janice: Kraft just put you in position to tie for second. Three questionable starters will decide whether you get there.

Mike E: two weeks in a row with a bye in your lineup. 36.52 points in, and your kicker’s team is off this week. You know what to do.
