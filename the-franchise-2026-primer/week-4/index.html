Thursday Night

Browns 27, Steelers 24.

Cleveland went up 14 in the first half and spent the rest of the night trying to give it back. Deshaun Watson threw for 268, fumbled twice and missed Harold Fannin Jr. on a late throw, and Pittsburgh made him pay. Aaron Rodgers went 22-of-40 for 299, three touchdowns and two picks, took five sacks, and still dragged the Steelers back. A Darnell Washington pass interference flag on fourth-and-10 kept the drive alive, and a two-point pass to Pat Freiermuth tied it at 24 with 1:42 left.

Then Andre Szmyt hit from 56 with ten seconds left. That’s three straight wins for Cleveland and sole possession of first in the AFC North, which is a sentence nobody had on their bingo card in September.

Five starters from this league played. Two beat their number clearly, one landed about where he should have, and two came up short. The difference from last week: nobody did enough damage to flip anything.

The Board

Five favorites, one median line.

Last week Thursday flipped three games. This week it just made the rich richer. The Live Proj median sits at 129.86, and the board splits cleanly around it: every favorite is over the line and every underdog is under it. The closest underdog is Njigba Please, 2.28 short. That makes this a ten-team, two-games-each week where the projections have five people winning both.

Projections have been wrong before, and in this league they’re wrong most weeks. Week 1 went 1-for-5. Here’s what has to go wrong for the underdogs.

Final Word

Five chalk. Three days left.

Week 3, Thursday flipped three games. Week 4, Thursday didn’t move anything. That’s usually when Sunday does.

If every favorite wins, Chris sits alone at 6–2, and six teams are bunched within a game of each other, from 5–3 to 4–4. That includes Janice and Rob, who would both lose twice. This league went 1-for-5 on favorites in Week 1, so nobody should be printing standings yet.

Mike S: Jefferson’s out. Sutton isn’t. You know what to do.

And TJ: Eddy Pineiro, starting kicker. Vikings, starting defense. Two straight weeks of empty slots, and this week all ten are filled. Proud of you.

See you at one.
