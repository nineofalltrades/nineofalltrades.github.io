The Franchise · 2026 Week 12 Primer · Thanksgiving Edition · By Mohn Jadden

Pass the
Turkey Leg.

In 1989 John Madden handed Reggie White a turkey leg after the Eagles shut out the Cowboys 27–0, and Thanksgiving football got its trophy. Thursday at 4:30 it’s Eagles at Cowboys again, the NFL has added a Wednesday night opener, and 37 of The Franchise’s 100 starters play before anybody touches the leftovers. J Squad has seven of its ten on the field Thursday; Njigba Please doesn’t have one until Sunday. Three weeks left, the top five a game apart, and three teams within 1.21 points of the median. Grab a plate.

↓ Kickoff is tonight
The Headline

Four days, five games, one turkey leg.

Madden’s rule was simple: the best player in the Thanksgiving game got a cooked turkey leg and took a bite of it on national television. A year later he gave one to Emmitt Smith and said he wished the bird had five more legs so the Cowboys’ offensive line could eat too, which is how the multi-legged turkey was born, and he carried the whole thing to FOX in 1994. This year the menu runs three days: Packers at Rams tonight on Netflix, the Thursday tripleheader of Bears–Lions, Eagles–Cowboys and Chiefs–Bills, then Broncos at Steelers on Black Friday. Sixteen games, no byes, no excuses.

Bears–Lions is the appetizer on TV and the main course in this league: 12 Franchise starters and 161.29 projected points, including D-Unit’s Jahmyr Gibbs against the Bears defense that TEAM HoldMyCHUBBBA is starting in the same matchup. J Squad has 82% of its projection done by Thursday night, Team KAS and Henrys Heros send ten starters between them into Thursday and Friday, and The Annexation of PR’s Matthew Stafford opens the whole week tonight with Puka Nacua and Davante Adams both out. By Friday night, most of this league will know roughly where it stands. Njigba Please will still be waiting for its first snap.
