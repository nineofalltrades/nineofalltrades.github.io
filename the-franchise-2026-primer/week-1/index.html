Early Games

Wednesday and Thursday, in the books.

Seahawks 13, Patriots 10 at Lumen Field. 49ers 27, Rams 7 in Melbourne. Both games are already shaping this week’s matchups — some managers are playing with a cushion, some are already dug in a hole.

Seahawks 13, Patriots 10 · Wed 9/9

Drew Lock was efficient (16/22, 187, a TD) but Jaxon Smith-Njigba was the story: 8 catches, 122 yards, a score. Drake Maye’s regular-season debut was rough — 178 yards and a touchdown undone by three interceptions. Rhamondre Stevenson salvaged the day for New England (51 rush + 44 rec yards).

49ers 27, Rams 7 · Thu 9/10, Melbourne

San Francisco made the season opener look easy in front of a sold-out crowd in Australia. Brock Purdy went 25/34 for 205 and three touchdowns to three different receivers, and the defense forced two turnovers for ten points off them. Quietly, rookie Kaelon Black (14 carries, 65 yards) nearly out-touched Christian McCaffrey (10 carries, 68 yards) in the same backfield — a workload split worth tracking past Week 1.

Key Stat — Mike E Jaxon Smith-Njigba’s 23.20 points beat his own projection by +6.79 — and it’s Sherlock’s entire early lead in this week’s biggest mismatch.
Key Stat — Curtis Matthew Stafford (6.80 actual vs. 20.43 proj) and Puka Nacua (9.90 vs. 16.45) missed projection by a combined −20.18 points in the same loss.
