The Franchise · 2026 Week 9 Primer · By Mohn Jadden

1,120 Days,
One Fleecing.

Nobody in this league had made a trade since October 12, 2023, and at 8:06 Thursday night Eric ended that by sending Zach Charbonnet to Njigba Please for Sam LaPorta. Black Hat Fantasy’s trade calculator calls it lopsided, 1,564 in market value against 844, and Mike E now doesn’t have a tight end on his roster. The trade didn’t save Eric’s Thursday, though, because Zay Flowers’ six receiving yards flipped The McClusterfucks to an 8.98 underdog against an Annexation of PR team with no kicker. If the chalk holds, TJ goes to 13–5 and Rob, sitting 0.12 under the median, falls a game behind him.

↓ It started in Baltimore
The Headline

Jaguars 33, Ravens 10.

With Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley both on IR, Baltimore gave an undrafted rookie his first start and tried to hide him by snapping the ball straight to Derrick Henry. Jacksonville coach Liam Coen said afterward they’d practiced against the Wildcat “a ton,” and it showed. The Jaguars went 77 yards on the opening drive for a Bhayshul Tuten touchdown, Trevor Lawrence hit Parker Washington for a 50-yard score with 58 seconds left in the half to make it 17–3, and Fagnano finished with 60 passing yards and two interceptions. Brissett is on his way from Arizona, which doesn’t help anyone this week.

Seven of our starters played. The Jacksonville side was mixed, and both Baltimore guys got buried. Henry got 57 yards on 17 carries and missed his number by 6.95. The scoreboard says TEAM HoldMyCHUBBBA leads D-Unit 20.20 to 13.10, but Chad is still an 11-point underdog, and the only favorite that actually changed hands was Eric’s.

The Trade

LaPorta for Charbonnet.

The last trade in The Franchise was a three-for-three on October 12, 2023: Justin Jefferson, Kenneth Walker and Rachaad White went to Team KAS, and Amon-Ra St. Brown, Jonathan Taylor and D’Andre Swift went to HoldMyChubb. Then nobody traded anybody for 1,120 days, which fits a league whose own chronicle says trades explain nothing about who wins. Here’s what the drought ended with.

Key StatThe market says this was a 46 percent fleecing. The scoreboard says it’s closer than that: LaPorta’s 70.50 points rank only seventh among tight ends, and the best tight end nobody in this league rosters is within two points of him over the same seven games. Most of that 1,564 is the name.

The case for Mike E. His backfield is a triage tent. Jeremiyah Love and James Conner are on IR, Cam Skattebo has missed three straight practices with a hamstring, and the healthy guys are Hampton at 9.4 a game and Rachaad White at 9.9. Charbonnet scored 12 touchdowns in 2025, Kenneth Walker is gone, and Blackhat already projects him higher this week than any receiver on Mike E’s bench. The Contrarian finally bought a running back, and the idea wasn’t crazy.

The case against. He paid in the one currency he didn’t have. The calculator flags his roster as WR-heavy, with Diggs, Downs, Boston, Douglas, Thomas and Deebo all sitting on the bench, and he sent away his only tight end instead. Charbonnet isn’t a lead back yet either: in his return he had 6 carries for 23 yards while rookie first-rounder Jadarian Price led Seattle’s backfield in snaps. Seattle’s bye is Week 11, which takes out Charbonnet and Jaxon Smith-Njigba on the same Sunday. And it’s a long-term swing from a 4–12 team that’s three wins out of sixth with the lowest points-for in the league. On Week 9 alone, Charbonnet over Diggs in the flex is worth about +1.9, and the zero at tight end costs about 10. The trade starts eight points in the hole.

Grade · The McClusterfucks (Eric)
B+
Eric won the market by 720 and got a top-six tight end for a running back who’s played one game this year. It isn’t an A because LaPorta (70.50) is basically Kelce (67.50) again, and Eric gave up a back the same week McCaffrey is questionable.
Grade · Njigba Please (Mike E)
D+
It was the right idea, paid with the wrong position, and so far the only thing it’s produced is a zero in the TE slot. The plus is for Charbonnet’s touchdown upside and for LaPorta being more replaceable than his price tag says.
