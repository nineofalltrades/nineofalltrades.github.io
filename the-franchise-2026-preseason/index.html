Verdict

Curtis paying premium for a wide receiver is not a new film. He owns the worst purchase in league history — $95 of Javonte Williams, −134 VOR — and then kept him. Nacua’s worth every dollar. That’s what makes it terrifying.

The villain protects two running backs and keeps $119. This is a man who bought Drake Maye for $5, the single best pickup in twelve years of league history. He does not need the money. He will find another one anyway.

The McClusterfucks
Eric · The commissioner
$76
RB Christian McCaffrey $46 WR CeeDee Lamb $30
Verdict

The commissioner spent $46 on a running back turning 30 and will spend Week 4 starting someone else instead. Four rings and four of the five worst sits ever recorded. Same guy. Same hands.

Nico’d & Dimed
TJ · The champ
$76
RB Bijan Robinson $52 TE Brock Bowers $24
Verdict

The champ protected the two safest weekly floors in the sport, which is sensible, because his documented hobby is leaving them in the garage. Last year’s ring came with 14.6 points a week on the bench and 27 points of Stafford watching from a folding chair.

Sherlock
Mike E · Zero running backs
$69
WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba $48 WR Malik Nabers $21
Verdict

Two receivers, zero running backs, and the cleanest lineup card this league has ever produced (93.6% efficiency) to run them through. The man you all accuse of never setting his flex is statistically the best starter among us. Keep saying it. It gets funnier every year.

Team KAS
Mike · The cleanest keep on the board
$60
WR Justin Jefferson $30 RB Ashton Jeanty $30
Verdict

The cleanest keep on the board. Two top-20 players, thirty apiece, $140 left over. No drama, no overpay, no headline — just the most starting production per dollar in the league. Nobody’s talking about it. They will Monday night.

Henrys Heros
Chris · Cheapest keeper on the board
$46
RB Derrick Henry $39 WR Marvin Harrison Jr. $7
Verdict

The longest lease in league history gets renewed again, and MHJ at seven dollars is the cheapest keeper anyone declared — a price that says the market has quietly filed him under “we tried.” Chris won a title in 2024 by 0.86 points without meaningfully touching the waiver wire. He does not care what the market thinks.

J Squad
Janice · Lightest bill, biggest war chest
$38
RB Kyren Williams $28 WR DJ Moore $10
Verdict

The lightest keeper bill and the biggest war chest — either the most disciplined move of the offseason or a quiet admission there was nothing else worth protecting. She went 12–2 in 2022 and still missed the final, so we all know exactly what a strong position is worth around here.
