🔒 THE FRANCHISE — 2026 KEEPERS ARE LOCKED

Ten teams. Twenty players. $681 of the league’s $2,000 spent before a single bid.

Twenty players are off the board. Ten of them run the ball. One of you spent more than half your cap before a single bid was placed, on two men who share a bye week.

💸 THE BOARD

Ordered by cap committed. Read it as a ranking of nerve.

D-Unit (Dao) — Jahmyr Gibbs ($57), Ja’Marr Chase ($50) → $107

Verdict: Fifty-four percent of his cap on two men — and Gibbs and Chase share a bye week. The genius who once leased $105 Saquon for six seasons goes dark in Week 6 with an $80 ceiling to fix it.

The Annexation of PR (Curtis) — Puka Nacua ($53), Chase Brown ($34) → $87

Verdict: Curtis paying premium for a wide receiver is not a new film. He owns the worst purchase in league history — $95 of Javonte Williams, −134 VOR — and then kept him. Nacua’s worth every dollar. That’s what makes it terrifying.

TEAM HoldMyCHUBBBA (Chad) — Jonathan Taylor ($45), James Cook III ($36) → $81

Verdict: The villain protects two running backs and keeps $119. This is a man who bought Drake Maye for $5, the single best pickup in twelve years of league history. He does not need the money. He will find another one anyway.

The McClusterfucks (Eric) — Christian McCaffrey ($46), CeeDee Lamb ($30) → $76

Verdict: The commissioner spent $46 on a running back turning 30 and will spend Week 4 starting someone else instead. Four rings and four of the five worst sits ever recorded. Same guy. Same hands.

Nico’d & Dimed (TJ) — Bijan Robinson ($52), Brock Bowers ($24) → $76

Verdict: The champ protected the two safest weekly floors in the sport, which is sensible, because his documented hobby is leaving them in the garage. Last year’s ring came with 14.6 points a week on the bench and 27 points of Stafford watching from a folding chair.

Sherlock (Mike E) — Jaxon Smith-Njigba ($48), Malik Nabers ($21) → $69

Verdict: Two receivers, zero running backs, and the cleanest lineup card this league has ever produced (93.6% efficiency) to run them through. The man you all accuse of never setting his flex is statistically the best starter among us. Keep saying it. It gets funnier every year.

Team KAS (Mike) — Justin Jefferson ($30), Ashton Jeanty ($30) → $60

Verdict: The cleanest keep on the board. Two top-20 players, thirty apiece, $140 left over. No drama, no overpay, no headline — just the most starting production per dollar in the league. Nobody’s talking about it. They will Monday night.

Henrys Heros (Chris) — Derrick Henry ($39), Marvin Harrison Jr. ($7) → $46

Verdict: The longest lease in league history gets renewed again, and MHJ at seven dollars is the cheapest keeper anyone declared — a price that says the market has quietly filed him under “we tried.” Chris won a title in 2024 by 0.86 points without meaningfully touching the waiver wire. He does not care what the market thinks.

Ken You Not Pickett (Rob) — Saquon Barkley ($33), Lamar Jackson ($8) → $41

Verdict: Saquon and the reigning MVP for forty-one dollars total — less than Dao paid for his cheaper man.

J Squad (Janice) — Kyren Williams ($28), DJ Moore ($10) → $38

Verdict: The lightest keeper bill and the biggest war chest — either the most disciplined move of the offseason or a quiet admission there was nothing else worth protecting. She went 12–2 in 2022 and still missed the final, so we all know exactly what a strong position is worth around here.

🧠 THE VERDICT

Best allocation

Team KAS. Two top-20 players at $30 each — the most starting production per dollar anyone bought, and the only manager who paid the same price twice.

Four men, more than a franchise

Gibbs $57, Nacua $53, Bijan $52, Chase $50 — $212 combined. A full sixteen-man roster costs $200. Four players in this league now cost more than a team.

Cheapest keep

Marvin Harrison Jr., $7. Chris’s entire keeper bill is $46 — less than Puka Nacua by himself. Either the shrewdest offseason anyone had or a very expensive shrug.

The position everyone bet on

$400 of the $681 spent — 59% — went to running backs. Ten of twenty keepers carry the ball. Chad is the only manager who kept two. Mike E is the only one who kept none.

Ten of you looked at the same board and reached ten different conclusions. Nine of you are wrong.
