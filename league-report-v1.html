A bug worth recording. The first version of this table had 9ofalltrades at 98%, three standard deviations clear of the league. It was wrong. Yahoo renders your own team page with 19 columns and every other team's with 20 — the extra one being "Action", the add/drop control you only get on your own roster. The parser read a fixed column index, so for one manager across all fifteen seasons it was reading projected points as actual. Projections are smooth, so the optimal lineup and the started lineup agreed almost perfectly and the number looked like genius. The parser now finds the column by header name, that manager's data was refetched, and the corrected figure is 88.12%.

What this report can't tell you yet

313 of 2,450 weekly roster fetches were lost to Yahoo rate limiting. Nothing was invented to fill them — those team-weeks are simply absent, which is why some managers show fewer seasons of lineup data than seasons played.

Draft value credits production, not possession. If a manager drafted a player, dropped him, and that player then scored elsewhere, VOR still credits the draft pick. This inflates the drafting reputation of anyone with heavy roster churn.

Weekly points are verified, season aggregates drift slightly. Our weekly totals reproduce Yahoo's official season points to 0.00% for 2011–2021. For 2022–2025 they run under by 0.9–3.3%, even though spot-checks of individual weeks match the rendered page exactly — so Yahoo aggregates the season on a slightly different basis than summing started players. Games decided by under a point should be treated with care in those four seasons.

2011 "kool aid" is unidentified. The account carries TJ's email in Yahoo's export but was a different person, cause unknown.
