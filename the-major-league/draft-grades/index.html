The Method

A projection, not a verdict.

This is a snake, not an auction. There is no price tag, so the currency is draft capital — the pick itself — measured against the field’s consensus opinion of that player (a blended ADP across CBS, ESPN, FFToday, FantasyPros, and Sleeper) and against Black Hat Fantasy’s own projected VOR at 1-point PPR, scaled to this league’s settings (10 teams, 1 QB / 2 RB / 3 WR / TE / FLEX / K / DEF).

Value vs. market is the projected VOR of the best player available at that exact overall pick, per the field’s ADP order, minus the VOR of the player actually taken. Negative is a bargain. Positive is a reach. Same sign convention as the auction report. Projected roster VOR is points over a replacement-level player at that position, summed across each team’s projected Week 1 starting lineup. No keeper math: this redraft does not carry keepers, so every pick here was earned in real time, in draft order.

Draft night is a head start, not a verdict. We grade the process now and settle the score in January.

One disclosureBlack Hat Fantasy’s own model does not buy Josh Jacobs at his consensus ADP (42nd overall) — it has him projected below replacement. He fell to pick 138 anyway, and somebody still took the swing. Grade him with your own risk tolerance, not just ours. See: The Defendants, LaddGPT.
