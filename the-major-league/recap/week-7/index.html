The Story

A Carolina Defense, a Tampa Running Back, and Eighty-Eight Hundredths

Al's winning streak ended in Charlotte, on a defense he'd owned for about 72 hours. The Kulps, meanwhile, finally stopped being tied.

Al Buys the Panthers at the Top

Al came into Week 7 on a three-game win streak, with the fewest points against in the league and a 14.21-point projected edge on the defending champ. On Thursday at 1:13 p.m. by Yahoo's clock, he dropped the Patriots defense for the Carolina Panthers. On Friday evening he dropped Patrick Mahomes for Bryce Young, also a Panther, presumably for the vibes. Then the Buccaneers went into Charlotte and won 43–13. Bryce threw two interceptions on Al's bench. Chuba Hubbard, another Panther, scored 10.00 on a 17.10 projection. And the Panthers D, the newest member of the family, allowed 35-plus and scored −4.00 on a 6.34 projection. That's a 10.34-point hole from one roster spot, in a game Al lost by 0.88.

The guy who ran through the hole was Bucky Irving, who plays for WhoseHungry?: 106 yards, two rushing touchdowns, 24.00 points. CeeDee Lamb caught two touchdowns for 24.40, Matthew Stafford threw three for 18.50, and a Saquon Barkley playing on a bad hamstring still found the end zone. Mike did this without Mike Evans, DeVonta Smith, Tyler Warren, Jeremiyah Love or Zach Charbonnet, all ruled out. Al, for his part, had Quinshon Judkins score two touchdowns and 23.40 on his bench, but Woody Marks was projected 3.61 points higher, so that's a bad beat, not a crime. The Panthers D is a crime. Final: 121.40–120.52. Al's only two sub-one-point losses in 15 seasons of this league's records have both come this year, Week 3 by 0.42 and now this. And the record Mike now owns, most points against through seven weeks, used to belong to Al's 2018 team, absALute dominationA (1,099.34). The champ broke Al's record, then broke Al's streak. Whether he was allowed to hold the trophy afterward is between him and his household.

The Kulps Stop Being Tied

Last week, pat and Brian Kulp were separated by 0.22 points through six weeks. This week Brian got Burrow, Gibbs and Jefferson back from the bye, scored 144.68 and beat GooNads by 53.00. Burrow threw for 28.88, Ashton Jeanty ran for 21.60, Colston Loveland added 19.90, and Gibbs scored 18.30 before tweaking an ankle late (Detroit expects him to be fine). He also did the thing he does now, which is set his lineup exactly backward from the week before. Last week he benched Germie Bernard (25.70) and started Rhamondre Stevenson (3.60). This week he started Bernard (5.70) and benched Rhamondre (22.50), with Luther Burden (23.70) and Caleb Williams (22.12) sitting next to him. He left 35.00 on the bench and still won by 53. Brian is now the league's bench champion for the season, and also in fourth place, which tells you how deep that bench is.

pat, meanwhile, got hit with the Buffalo blackout this column warned about and did everything he could about it. Thursday morning, 7:54 to 7:57 a.m.: Trevor Lawrence out for Tyler Shough, Keon Coleman out for Ja'Kobi Lane, Tyler Bass out for Evan McPherson. It didn't matter. All six of his bench players, James Cook, Ray Davis, DJ Moore, Omarion Hampton, Keaton Mitchell and Rachaad White, were on bye. His lineup was technically perfect, because there was nothing left to pick from. Then Garrett Wilson scored 4.60 on a 16.68 projection, Sam LaPorta 2.80 at flex, TreVeyon Henderson 4.30 and McPherson 5.00. The total was 81.42, the second-lowest score of the season, and the gap between the Kulps is now 63.04. Next week, Shough, Chris Olave and the Saints defense are all on bye. pat is a 72.74-point projection. Thanksgiving's going to be quieter than we thought.
