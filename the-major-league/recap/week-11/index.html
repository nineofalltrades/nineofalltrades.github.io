The Story

A Receiver Who Caught Nothing, and an Al Who Can't Lose

One game came down to 74 hundredths of a point. Another one sent the league's least likely playoff team into December with a reservation.

Courtland Sutton Caught Nothing. Natalie Won by 0.74.

Denver beat Las Vegas 38-24 on Sunday. Jonah Coleman ran for three touchdowns, Evan Engram caught six balls, and Courtland Sutton was targeted twice and caught zero. It was the first time in 39 games he'd been blanked. He was in GooNads' flex spot, projected for 9.10, and he gave her 0.00. Natalie won anyway, because Dak Prescott threw four touchdowns and ran for another in the Cowboys' 51-30 win over Tennessee (35.90, the best player score in the league). Derrick Henry added 20.10, and the Ravens defense gave up 35-plus points and still scored 9.00 on a return touchdown. Jalen Coker left with a concussion after 8.70. Her bench had nothing that could play flex: J.K. Dobbins scored 0.00, Kincaid was out, Michael Wilson is on IR, and Jared Goff's 20.36 was stuck behind Dak. So 119.20 was the best lineup her roster could field. It was a perfect lineup with a zero in it.

Ryan was Yahoo's 7.39-point favorite, and then Josh Allen threw two interceptions against Miami (14.16, 9.50 under projection) and Chase Brown scored 8.60. The bench had what he needed. The 49ers defense scored 15.00 there while he started the Broncos (13.00), and Braelon Allen scored 9.40 while Travis Etienne started (7.60). Either swap alone would have won it. Both sat because they were projected lower, so neither one is a lineup mistake. Ryan lost the season's second-closest game by less than the 1.80-point gap between two running backs on his own roster. Natalie's four-game losing streak is over and she's out of last place. Ryan is 6-5 and still holds the last playoff spot. The trophy remains officially Mike's. Natalie was in a good enough mood Sunday night that people are asking where she was keeping it.

Al Has Clinched. Al.

In 2025, as ALBERTosaurus, Al went 0-14 in the regular season and finished last for the second year in a row. Through 11 weeks that team had allowed 1,695.52 points, the third-most at that point of a season in league history. He hasn't finished in the top six since 2020. On Sunday he beat TJ 155.30–113.44 for his fourth straight win, moved to 8-3, and Yahoo put the asterisk next to his name. Al is in the playoffs with three weeks left, and his 1,266.52 points against is the fewest in the league. That's 429 fewer than last year's team had allowed by this point.

He did it the boring way. Chuba Hubbard ran for 120 and two scores (26.00), Javonte Williams scored twice (24.10), George Pickens caught seven for 102 and a touchdown (23.80), Purdy threw three touchdowns (22.20), Kittle caught two of them (18.40), and Boswell kicked three field goals (13.00). Al left 20.60 on his bench, the most in the league, and won by 41.86. Brian clinched the same afternoon. He beat Kevin 157.00–93.76 for his fifth straight win, with Jonah Coleman's three touchdowns (34.90) out of the flex spot. The two clinched teams own the two best records and the league's two longest active win streaks, five and four.

Awards

This Week's Hardware

Nobody started a player who was projected 3 or more points below someone on the bench, and nobody started anyone who'd been ruled out. Every bench bomb this week was a bad beat. The awards had to find other material.

👻 The Ghost Flex Award

Natalie, who won a game with Courtland Sutton's 0.00 in her flex spot. It wasn't even a mistake: the only bench points she had were Jared Goff's 20.36, and he's a quarterback. A perfect lineup with a hole in it, and a 0.74-point win.

📐 The Right Call, Wrong Sunday Award

Ryan, who correctly started the Broncos over the 49ers and Etienne over Braelon Allen, then watched the benched pair outscore the started pair by 3.80 in a 0.74-point loss. He even nailed the Vele call. Process: A. Result: 6-5.

🔒 The Reservation Confirmed Award

Al, clinched at 8-3, one calendar year after 0-14. Brian clinched too, but Brian has three titles and Al hasn't finished in the top six since 2020, so Al gets the big font.

🕰️ The 12:28 Club

Mike and TJ each made a Sunday free-agent move at exactly 12:28 p.m. by Yahoo's clock. Mike got Cam Ward and 23.22. TJ got Brenton Strange and 4.60. Same minute, 18.62 points apart, and only one of them won.

🚪 The Early Exit Award

DK Metcalf, ejected in Philadelphia after two run-ins with Cooper DeJean. He says he wasn't going for the eye. Either way he left with one catch for two yards, and Mike's WR2 slot was the real victim.

🧹 The Perfect and Pointless Award

Kevin, who set the optimal lineup and scored 93.76, the week's low, while four of his guys sat on bye. Honorable mention to TJ, also perfect, also beaten by 41-plus. A perfect lineup is only as good as the roster behind it.

🔄 The No Returns Award

Michaeopteryx, who dropped Drake Maye (ankle, out indefinitely) for Kirk Cousins on Wednesday. Cousins threw for 235, no touchdowns and an interception. The logic was fine. The result was Kirk Cousins.
