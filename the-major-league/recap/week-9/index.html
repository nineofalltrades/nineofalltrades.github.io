The Story

A Quarterback Comes Home, a Receiver Goes Down, and the Champ Wins Into the Record Book

Three stories, and each one turned on who was healthy enough to play.

Kevin Got His Quarterback Back. Brian Got 0.18 of a Point.

Last week's order was "Fix that, Kevin." Consider it fixed. C.J. Stroud came off his bye and scored 36.36, 0.06 clear of Kenneth Walker for the best player score in the league. Add Chase's 31.50 (ten catches, 155 yards in Madrid) and Watson's 30.10, and those three made 97.96 of BlueGreen's 159.56. Kevin left 1.20 on the bench, beat pat by 44.46, and went from fifth to second, 59.16 points better than a week ago.

It was still the second-best score. CadillacKulpDeville went 159.74 on a Burrow Madrid game (271 yards, three touchdown passes, one rushing score, 29.54) and a 32.40 from Matthew Golden at WR3. Brian left 15.70 on the bench, Luther Burden's 21.70 among it, and still beat Van Tine by 66.18. Ryan's 93.56 was the week's low.

Al Was Perfect. Then Came Puka's Knee.

Al set the only perfect lineup in the league: 147.38 out of 147.38. Puka Nacua gave him 36.00 (14 catches, 150 yards, a touchdown, on 19 of Matthew Stafford's 43 targets, so Mike's quarterback fed Al's receiver), Purdy added 26.28, and Greatest_of_AL_time beat Human Psentipede by 36.82. At 6-3 it's Al's best start since 2020. A year ago at this point he was 0-9.

Then Puka hurt his knee on a 5-yard run with 1:44 left, the Rams' last offensive snap. The MRI was Monday, the team doesn't believe it's a torn ACL, and I couldn't find a result as of Tuesday morning. Davante Adams is out after hamstring surgery. Al's other receivers are Egbuka (6.90) and Antonio Williams (5.50). Send a waiver claim.

Mike Wins the Marriage Bowl and Keeps the Wrong Crown

WhoseHungry? beat GooNads 144.52–97.18, Mike's 19th win in 25 meetings. Lamb went for 34.50, Irving ran for 28.50 and Fairbairn kicked 17.00. And Mike's points against still went up: Natalie's 97.18 took him to 1,383.22, which breaks the nine-week record of 1,381.58 that Kevin's 2018 Blue Green set. He needed 95.54. His wife gave him 97.18. He wins and gets scored on, which is the most Mike thing that has ever happened, and he now leads the league in points against by 131.44.

The trophy, meanwhile, is officially Mike's, though the season-long rumor says Natalie has been hiding it since last year's title game. Nothing turned up this week. GooNads is 2-7 on a three-game skid. On suspicion, the trophy remains at large.
