The Story

A Free Agent Throws Five, a Shootout Pays Al, and the Champ Loses His Only Record

Two games produced most of the points in this league on Sunday. Haefner owned one. Al owned the other.

The Waiver Wire Had Patrick Mahomes On It. Haefner Noticed.

Haefner already had Jayden Daniels. On Wednesday morning he dropped Devin Singletary and added Patrick Mahomes anyway, straight out of free agency. Kenneth Walker hurt his groin in practice and was ruled out Saturday, which handed the Chiefs' backfield to fifth-round rookie Emmett Johnson, also on Haefner's roster. Mahomes went 22 of 35 for 348 yards and five touchdowns in Atlanta. Two went to Johnson and one went to Josh Downs, and both of them were in Haefner's lineup too. Johnson ran for 101, caught six for 70, and scored 35.10. Mahomes scored 35.42. Add Downs (15.30) and the Chiefs defense Haefner also picked up off the wire (11.00), and Kansas City put 96.82 points into one fantasy lineup.

Then the rest of the lineup showed up. Smith-Njigba caught eight for 171 (31.10), Parker Washington went for 152 (28.20), and Isaiah Likely, a backup tight end in most people's minds, caught nine for 127 (27.70). The total was 219.02, with zero points on the bench. That's the best score in the league this season by 26.76, Haefner's best since a 227.20 in Week 15 of 2015, the fifth-highest score in league history, and the third-highest perfect lineup anybody has ever set. GooNads scored 144.42, the fourth-best total of the week, and lost by 74.60. Natalie didn't do anything wrong except have Haefner on the schedule.

49ers 46, Cowboys 40, Al 122.16

The late game in Arlington was the kind of shootout where the defenses should have been fined. Brock Purdy threw for 389 and three scores (31.66). George Pickens caught 11 for 145 and three touchdowns (43.50). Deebo Samuel scored once rushing and once receiving (25.90), Kittle added 13.40 and Javonte Williams 7.70. Every one of them was in Al's starting lineup. That's 122.16 points from one game, 64% of Greatest_of_AL_time's 189.76. Chuba Hubbard tacked on 34.20 in New Orleans for good measure.

Kevin was Yahoo's 11.45-point favorite and lost by 59.72. Ja'Marr Chase caught 11 balls for 231 yards (34.10) and it barely registered. Al is 7-3 on a three-game streak, his best record through ten weeks since he went 8-2 in 2020. A year ago at this point he was 0-10. The same shootout fed Natalie, too: Dak Prescott threw five touchdowns (35.42) and McCaffrey caught 161 yards' worth of passes on his way to 46.30, the best player score of the week. She lost anyway, which is the theme of her autumn.

Mike Loses the One Thing He Was Winning

Through nine weeks WhoseHungry? had been scored on more than any team in league history. Through ten, Kevin's 2018 Blue Green has the record back at 1,563.08. To keep it, Mike needed Ryan to put up 179.87. Ryan scored 140.94, which was enough to beat Mike by 34.92 and not enough to give him the record. Mike's 1,524.16 now ranks third all-time, behind Kevin and Mike's own 2019 team. He still holds two of the top three. He's 3-7.

Mike's byes didn't help: Irving, Barkley and DeVonta Smith all sat, and Tyler Warren (2.40) and Allgeier (1.40) didn't step in. The trophy is still officially his. The rumor that Natalie keeps hiding it remains a rumor, and she's now lost four straight, which is either a cover story or a very committed bit.

Awards

This Week's Hardware

One genuine lineup blunder this week, and it cost exactly nothing.

🏷️ The Clearance Rack Award

Justin Haefner, who found Patrick Mahomes in free agency on Wednesday and the Chiefs defense there the week before. Together they scored 46.42, and neither cost him anything but a roster spot. The rest of the league is refreshing the free-agent list as we speak.

🎆 The Fireworks Dividend Award

Al, who started five players from a 46-40 game and collected 122.16 points from it. Natalie had three players in the same game and got 89.72, which is how you score 144.42 and lose.

🪑 The Empty Chair Award

Natalie, who started Carnell Tate at wide receiver after the Titans ruled him out with a shoulder injury. Every receiver on her bench was on bye or IR, so the fix was the waiver wire. It's the only real lineup mistake of the week, and she lost by 74.60, so it cost nothing but this award.

👑 The Abdication Award

Mike, who lost the ten-week points-against record when Ryan scored only 140.94 on him. The record needed 179.87. Mike was out-scored by enough to lose and not by enough to stay historic.

🎁 The Regift Award

TJ, who cut Cam Little for Jake Bates on November 4. Ryan claimed Little last Wednesday. Sunday: Little 19.00, Bates 5.00. TJ won anyway, so the only casualty is his kicker judgment.

🏥 The M*A*S*H Unit Award

Drake Maye (ankle, first drive; Michaeopteryx), Brock Bowers (ankle; TJ), A.J. Brown (hamstring; Natalie), Kenneth Walker (groin, ruled out Saturday; Haefner) and Puka Nacua (knee sprains, now on IR; Al). Two of the five managers won by 59 or more anyway.
