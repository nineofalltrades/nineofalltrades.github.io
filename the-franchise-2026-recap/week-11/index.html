The Franchise · Week 11 · By Mohn Jadden

Foreclosure.

Week 11 was pure chalk: five favorites, five wins, zero upsets on Yahoo’s numbers or Blackhat’s, and the standings still got flipped like a card table at Thanksgiving. Dao went from fourth to first on a rookie’s three touchdowns. TJ beat last place by 44.56 and fell two spots. Njigba Please scored 65.80 and got eliminated. Three weeks left, and one game separates the top five.

The Headline

Won the Game, Lost the House

Last week we said if the timeshare couldn’t beat Njigba Please, it was going into foreclosure. TJ beat Njigba Please by 44.56 and got foreclosed anyway. His 110.36 finished seventh of ten, 7.91 under the median, so the defending champ went 1–1 against the worst team in the league and slid from first to third. Meanwhile Dao, fourth a week ago, posted the week’s best score, won his fifth straight head-to-head game and took first on points, of which he has 202.14 more than TJ.

Now one game separates the top five: Dao and TJ at 14–8, Chad, Rob and Janice at 13–9. Chad jumped from fifth to second and grabbed the Weak Side lead, while Rob and Janice, tied for first last week, both went 0–2. TJ didn’t miss a payment. The guy down the hall has just averaged 185.35 over his last four weeks.

🏆 Weekly Hardware

🪑 Best Seat in the House, Part Four

Patrick Mahomes scored 30.66 on Janice’s bench, the most on any bench in the league. She started Dak, won that coin flip by 11.44, and lost everything else.

📉 The Big Two

Ja’Marr Chase and Jaylen Warren combined for 10.40 on a 36.25 projection, and Dao won by 45.80 anyway. That’s not depth, that’s a different league.

🎟️ Wrong Week, Right Score

Team KAS scored 141.46, enough to beat seven teams, and left Ladd McConkey’s 18.20 in the IR slot. Swap him in and they still lose by 2.20.

🐐 Bench Goat

Rob’s $2 receiver, Antonio Williams, scored 17.70 on the bench while his $5 receiver, Ryan Flournoy, scored 1.60 in the lineup. Flournoy had the better projection; Williams had the better afternoon.

🔁 The Braelon Allen Boomerang

Eric cut Braelon Allen Wednesday for an $11 Kyle Monangai. Rob claimed Allen for $3 and started him for 10.50; Monangai scored 2.60 on Eric’s bench.

✂️ End of an Era

Chad cut Drake Maye at 6:06 p.m. Friday by Yahoo’s clock and added Cam Ward one minute later. Ward scored 24.52 on his bench, because some relationships never leave the bench.

🏃 The 37-Hour Tight End

Janice paid $4 for Pat Freiermuth on Wednesday morning, cut him Thursday night for T.J. Hockenson, then started Mark Andrews over both. That’s not a tight end room, it’s a revolving door.

Start/Sit Court: Janice, please rise. Kenneth Walker sat out Sunday and stayed in your RB slot for 0.00. Your bench couldn’t help (Woody Marks was out with a concussion, Kyren Williams was on bye), but the waiver wire could: Justice Hill was sitting there and scored 16.20, and Ty Johnson scored 15.10. Any running back who scored 1.87 would have cleared the median and made it a 1–1 week instead of 0–2. Guilty of leaving a dead slot with the fix one click away.

Mohn's Closing Thought

Every favorite finally won and it still rearranged half the table. The top five are a game apart, the sixth seed runs through a brothers’ game, and the only settled address in this league is the basement. Three weeks left. Pick your poison.
