The Headline

Fifty-Three Point Joe

The Bengals and Titans went to overtime in Cincinnati, 43–36, and half this league was standing in the blast radius. Joe Burrow went 38 of 48 for 462 yards and five touchdowns, threw three interceptions to the same safety, then scrambled nine yards for the overtime winner. That makes him the second quarterback in NFL history with 450 passing yards, five touchdown passes and a rushing score in one game, and the first was Norm Van Brocklin in 1951, when fantasy football was a guy at a bar saying he could’ve been a contender. Burrow scored 53.58, the best line by any player in this league all season by 9.36, and Ja’Marr Chase added 26.60 for the same owner. The same game handed Eric 30.30 from Tony Pollard and Chris 33.50 from Tee Higgins, and both of them lost. Cincinnati threw candy like a parade. Only Dao brought a bag.

D-Unit hung 188.38 on Curtis and won by 59.10, the second-biggest margin of the season. Dao jumped from eighth to fifth, got back to .500, and took the league lead in points from TJ: 1,116.44. Last week the question was whether his scoring or his record was lying, and it turns out the record was. Curtis is 0–4 across his last two weekends and the series he had tied is now 13–12 Dao. Curtis didn’t lose to D-Unit. He got invoiced by Ohio.
