The Squirt Gun
Heist.

Week 4 took the projections, put them in a blender, and hit puree. The biggest favorite of the week lost to a team that was missing its best receiver and got a zero from another one. The team with the worst score in Week 3 put up the best score of the year. The defending champ left a small village of points on his bench and still won in a landslide. If you saw any of this coming, you're lying.

Mike S pulled off a heist with half a crew. Dao found out a 35-point cushion is mostly air. Chad stopped pretending to be a fraud and started pretending to be a dynasty. Janice moved into first and didn't even have to get up. Eric made the right call at quarterback and still lost. Chris picked the wrong quarterback again and still won. Curtis lost a family argument, Mike E got run over, and Rob lost his quarterback and half his backfield by halftime. TJ won by a mile with his two hottest players watching. Let's get into it.

The Story

The Squirt Gun Heist

Friday afternoon, Team KAS was the deadest man walking in the league. Justin Jefferson was ruled out. Ladd McConkey was a game-time decision with a bad foot and bad ribs. D-Unit was favored by 35.50, the biggest spread on the board, and Dao still had Burrow, Gibbs and Chase to play. Mike S's own projection had him at 109. Bookies would've taken this one off the board out of pity.

Then Sunday happened. McConkey left early with the foot and finished with a 0.00. Ja'Marr Chase took a concussion and left with 4.20. Joe Burrow threw for 428 yards like a man trying to win the game by himself, put up 36.92, and the other nine guys in Dao's lineup combined for 71.80. Meanwhile Javonte Williams punched in three touchdowns in Houston and dropped 30.70. Dao went into Monday night down 12.80 with one bullet left: Juwan Johnson against the Falcons. He needed 12.81. He got 8.90. Final: 112.62–108.72. Mike S started a receiver who scored zero, had his best receiver on IR, and won anyway. That's not luck. That's a guy who wandered into a bank with a squirt gun and walked out with the vault.

Awards

🏆 Weekly Hardware

🎁 The Regift Award

TJ dropped Dontayvion Wicks on Tuesday, then paid $2 to get him back at 12:28 a.m. Friday, and cut Romeo Doubs to make room. Wicks: 3.30. Doubs, on waivers, belonging to nobody: two touchdowns and 20.80. TJ paid two dollars to swap a 20-point receiver for a 3-point one. After midnight. Nothing good happens after midnight.

📺 The Reunion Special

Travis Kelce spent nine seasons on Janice's roster. This week he finally faced her, in Eric's lineup, and gave his old boss 2.50 points of pure loyalty. Meanwhile Harold Fannin outscored him from Eric's bench for the second straight week. On paper it's a coin flip. On the scoreboard it's a sequel.

🪙 The Coin Flip Award

Last week Chris started Goff and Tyler Shough threw four touchdowns on his bench. This week he started Shough and Goff threw for 412 on his bench, 30.68 points of it. Both weeks the projections were about a tenth of a point apart. Chris has now been wrong both ways and won both games. Some guys are just built different.

🏦 The Interest-Bearing Account

Janice has not spent one dollar of FAAB this season. Not one. Seven teams have been burning FAAB like day traders, and the lady with all $100 still in the envelope is sitting in first place. Turns out the best waiver move is the one you don't make.

Start/Sit Court: nobody screwed up, three weeks running. Monangai, Wilson, Goff, Maye and Kamara all boomed on benches, and every one of them was projected below, or within a point of, the guy who started. Every ugly line this week was an injury or a bad beat. Court adjourned. Again.

Week 5

🚨 Storylines to Watch

1. The Season High vs. the First Seat

TEAM HoldMyCHUBBBA vs. J Squad. Chad just posted the best score of the year. Janice is in first on a tiebreaker. One of them is about to find out whether last week meant anything.

2. The Points Machine vs. the Commish

Nico'd & Dimed vs. The McClusterfucks. The league's top offense against a commissioner who just lost his flex to a hamstring and has $53 to replace him. TJ, meanwhile, has to keep an eye on Monangai's thumb.

3. The Infirmary Bowl

D-Unit vs. Ken You Not Pickett. Chase is in the concussion protocol. Saquon's hamstring is a question. Lamar is day-to-day. Whoever has more guys standing on Sunday morning probably wins.

4. Loser Goes to 3–7

The Annexation of PR vs. Team KAS. Two 3–5 teams, one season on the line. Mike S just pulled off the heist of the year, and Jefferson's hoping to be back. Curtis would like a turn at being lucky.

5. David vs. Goliath, Minus the Slingshot

Henrys Heros vs. Njigba Please. 6–2 against 2–6. Mike E needs this one badly, and he gets a team built around a 32-year-old running back who refuses to get old. Good luck.

Mohn's Closing Thought

Four weeks in, the team with the most points is third, the team that spends nothing is first, and the biggest lock of the season lost to a guy whose top receiver was on IR and whose fill-in scored zero. Projections are a suggestion. The median is a mood. Nobody in this league knows anything, and that includes me.

Special Shoutouts:

  • Mike S, for paying $30 to keep Justin Jefferson and getting his best win of the year the one week Jefferson didn't play.
  • Dao, for rostering two franchise quarterbacks and finding out that's still only one position.
  • Chad, for the most Chad week imaginable: the best score of the year with a tight end who scored 1.20.
  • Rob, for still not spending a single FAAB dollar with half the roster in the trainer's room. Discipline or denial, we'll find out.
  • Curtis, for doing everything right in a 3–5 season. Somewhere, a projection model is proud of you.
  • TJ, for making 14 waiver moves in four weeks. The wire has started sending him holiday cards.

Set those Week 5 lineups, read the injury report twice, and may your starters stay healthy, your bench stay quiet, and your opponent stay a 35-point favorite right up until kickoff.
