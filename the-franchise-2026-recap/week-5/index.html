Somebody turned off the defense in Week 5 and lost the switch. ONE GAME CLEARED FOUR HUNDRED POINTS, and the team that lost it would have beaten every other team in the league. The Story

404: Defense Not Found

Rob walked into Week 5 holding a roster that looked like a hospital discharge list. Lamar Jackson got ruled out Friday. Caleb Williams, his only other quarterback, was already out. Saquon and Josh Jacobs didn't play, Achane is done for the year, and Alec Pierce is on IR. So Rob went to the wire, and a $0 claim cleared at 12:58 a.m. Saturday: Kyler Murray. The same Kyler Murray that Curtis had cut at 4:29 a.m. Wednesday to make room for Keon Coleman. Kyler gave Rob 21.66. Keon gave Curtis 7.20, on the bench.

And then the waiting room got up and played. Nine of Rob's ten starters beat their projection. Bucky Irving ran for 165 on Thursday. Christian Watson went for 143 and two scores, Isaiah Likely caught 11 balls and two touchdowns, Mike Evans dropped 23.80 at flex. A team projected for 119.24 put up 197.06, which would have beaten every other team in the league by at least 38 points. The problem was Dao. Jahmyr Gibbs went for 207 yards and two touchdowns, Jaylen Warren scored on an 80-yard catch and a 50-yard run, Parker Washington caught two touchdowns, and D-Unit hung 207.20, the first 200-point week of the season. Rob lost by 10.14. Rob, you didn't lose a fantasy game. You lost a track meet to a guy on a motorcycle.

The Numbers That Matter

Top Team D-Unit — 207.20

The old season high was 172.68. Dao beat it by more than 34. Last week this man lost as a 35-point favorite. This week he needed every one of these points and used them all. Dao, the league owes you an apology. Rob does not.

Bench Goat TEAM HoldMyCHUBBBA — 38.22

Drake Maye threw for 30.16 on Chad's bench last week and 45.76 there this week. Both weeks he projected right around Trevor Lawrence, so it's a coin flip, not a crime. Kyle Pitts (12.70) and Brenton Strange (10.30) also sat, and either one would have flipped a 4.32-point loss. Maye has now scored 75.92 points for Chad in two weeks, and none of them counted. That's not a backup quarterback. That's a savings account with no withdrawals.

Unluckiest Ken You Not Pickett — lost with 197.06

The league average is about 130 a week. Rob's opponents are averaging 152.27 against him. He's 4–1 against the median and 1–4 against actual human beings, and he's given up 36 more points than anyone else in the league. Every manager in this league plays their best game against Rob. It's like he's the boss fight.

Awards

🏆 Weekly Hardware

🎁 The Regift Award, Part Two

Last week TJ cut Romeo Doubs and Doubs scored 20.80 on waivers, belonging to nobody. This week Chad paid $7 for him and started him at flex. Doubs: 2.10. Romeo Doubs only shows up when nobody owns him. He's a cat.

🚗 The Rental Car Award

Mike S picked up Tre' Harris at 7:13 a.m. Friday, started him Sunday, watched his hamstring go, and dropped him at 2:35 a.m. Tuesday. Four days, one start, returned with damage. Brutal, not his fault. Mike S won anyway and didn't even pay the insurance.

🚪 The Revolving Door Award

Justin Herbert, cut. Bryce Young, cut. Jacoby Brissett, a $0 claim, started: 28.88, which beat TJ's Brock Purdy by 9.32. Eric's third starting quarterback in three weeks is the best one yet. The commissioner's QB room now has a turnstile and a greeter.

🤝 The Everybody Ties Award

Jayden Daniels ran for 103 yards and two touchdowns, put up 35.86, then took a knee to end a 27–27 tie. All on TJ's bench, behind Purdy, who projected 0.07 higher. Daniels gave the Commanders and the Giants a tie and gave TJ a bad beat. TJ won by 34.88 anyway, so nobody's sending flowers.

💸 The $17 Foot

Mike E's $17 was the biggest bid of the week, on Will Shipley. Shipley started, scored a touchdown, and left with a foot injury. Seventeen dollars for six touches and a spot on the injury report. Gallows humor only. He still won by 37.

Start/Sit Court: no crimes, four weeks running. Every bench boom projected within three points of the guy who started, so nobody gets convicted. But the jury has questions.

Curtis, please rise. You lost by 2.08. Alvin Kamara gave you 1.90 at flex. Sitting on your bench: Emeka Egbuka (14.30), Keon Coleman (7.20) and Keaton Mitchell (5.00). Any one of the three wins you the game. Keaton. Mitchell. Five points. That's all it took. Not guilty, but we're keeping your photo on the wall.

Chad, you too. Swap Drake Maye in for Trevor Lawrence and that 4.32-point loss is a 13-point win. The projections had them neck and neck. The neck snapped.

Everyone else is free to go. Rob and Dao had nothing on their benches that would've moved a decimal, and Eric and Chris couldn't have covered their margins if they'd started the whole bench. Court adjourned.

Mohn's Closing Thought

Five weeks in, here's what we know: nothing. You control who you start and which waiver claims you put in. Everything after kickoff belongs to the football gods, and this week the football gods unplugged every defense in the country and sat back with popcorn.

Special Shoutouts:

  • Dao, for keeping Jalen Hurts on the bench as the fanciest spare tire in the league.
  • Janice, for the tidiest lineup of the week: one point left on her bench, and she didn't even need that.
  • Chad, for building a roster so deep his best player can't find a lineup spot.
  • TJ, for picking up Tyreek Hill and holding him like a lottery ticket. Still 0.00. Still scratching.
  • Mike E, for naming his team after Jaxon Smith-Njigba and getting 21.40 out of him. Brand synergy.
  • Chris, for MarShawn Lloyd's 17.20 on the bench, projected under the guy who started. Another bad beat for the collection.

Set those Week 6 lineups, read the injury report twice, and may your starters stay healthy, your bench stay quiet, and your opponent forget that defense is optional.
