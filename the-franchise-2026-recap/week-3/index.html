The Franchise · Week 3 · By Mohn Jadden

Hurts
So Good.

Week 3 was a bar fight where everybody threw a punch and the guy who got knocked out was holding the biggest stick. The two best teams in the league played each other and one of them had to go home a loser. The last unbeaten team lost twice in one weekend. The last winless team won twice. And the highest-scoring game of the week got decided by an offense that scored seven points on Monday night. Football is a beautiful, stupid sport.

Dao and TJ put on the show of the season and only one of them gets paid. Mike E found out that love is free. Janice found out what losing feels like, twice. Rob beat his brother on a day his training room would rather forget. Chris moved into the penthouse. Chad's record finally met his point total. Mike S is out of answers, Curtis is exactly average, and Eric is filing paperwork from the basement. Let's get into it.

The Story

Hurts So Good

This one was a heavyweight fight from the opening bell. Bijan Robinson went for 219 yards and two scores on Thursday night and dropped 38.20 for TJ. Dao answered Sunday with Jahmyr Gibbs: three touchdowns, 164 yards, 39.90, best game in the league. Ja'Marr Chase and Garrett Wilson both cleared 20. By Sunday night the two of them had combined for more points than some of you will score in October, and TJ was up 2.28 with one game left: Eagles at Bears. His DeVonta Smith against Dao's Jalen Hurts. Winner take all.

The Eagles scored SEVEN POINTS. That's it. Their one touchdown was a Hurts quarterback sneak from the 1, and the catch that set it up at the 1? DeVonta Smith. TJ's own receiver teed up the play that beat him. Hurts even left for a concussion check and came back to finish the job. Final: 159.32–155.48, and 314.80 combined in a game somebody had to lose. Dao goes from 1–3 to 3–3. TJ goes back to the waiver wire, which at this point is basically his second home.

Studs & Duds

The Numbers That Matter

Top Team D-Unit — 159.32

Second-best score of the season, 0.34 short of the record. Dao's reward for the best week of anybody's year? A .500 record. This league is a cruel place.

Bench Goat Henrys Heros — 26.24

Tyler Shough threw four touchdowns on the bench, and Terry McLaurin and Luther Burden both cleared 16 next to him. Shough and Goff projected a tenth of a point apart, so no foul. Chris won by 18.70 anyway and didn't even notice.

Unluckiest Nico'd & Dimed — lost with 155.48

That score beats every other team in the league by at least 25. TJ has now given up 437.48 points, most in the league by 22. Every week his opponent decides to have the game of their life. Take it as a compliment, TJ. Or don't.

Awards

🏆 Weekly Hardware

🪃 The Boomerang Award

Dao cut RJ Harvey and paid $35 for Jonah Coleman on Wednesday. By Saturday, Coleman was on IR and Dao was claiming Harvey back for $0. That's thirty-five dollars for three days of a guy on IR. The Airbnb of FAAB moves.

♻️ The Castoff Award, Part II

Janice cut Kittle in Week 2. Curtis paid $10 for him. This week Kittle scored two touchdowns for Curtis, and Janice lost her game by 12.40. The math on that one is going to keep her up tonight.

🛒 The Tight End Clearance Rack

Mike S paid $18 for Dalton Schultz. Chris paid $10 for Jake Ferguson. They started them head to head. Ferguson 7.80, Schultz 4.50. Mike S paid 80% more for 42% fewer points. Always check the sale rack, Mike.

🎟️ The Eight-Dollar Spectator

TJ spent $8 on Adonai Mitchell. Return on investment so far: 0.00. At least the two-dollar guy is still working.

📦 Fresh Out of the Box

Chad picked up Rashod Bateman Wednesday and slid him right into the flex Sunday. Bateman returned the favor with 4.70. Some things are better left in the packaging.

Start/Sit Court: nobody screwed up, two weeks running. Three benched quarterbacks outscored the guys who started over them this week, and every one of those starts was the right call on paper. Everything else ugly was an injury or a bad beat. Court adjourned.

Week 4

🚨 Storylines to Watch

1. Cousins, First Place on the Line

The Annexation of PR vs. Henrys Heros. Curtis and Chris, family, head to head, with the top of the table at stake. Somebody's mom is going to hear about this.

2. The Points Machine vs. the Median Monster

Nico'd & Dimed vs. Ken You Not Pickett. The league's highest scorer against the league's most beat-up contender, now starting life without Achane. One of these guys finally gets a break.

3. The Basement vs. the Bank Vault

The McClusterfucks vs. J Squad. The commish in last place against a team that won't spend a dime and just lost twice. Somebody's panic button is getting pressed.

4. Fraud Check, Part II

TEAM HoldMyCHUBBBA vs. Njigba Please. The lowest-scoring team in the league against the hottest one. Chad needs to prove last week was a fluke. The first three weeks say otherwise.

5. Streak vs. Skid

D-Unit vs. Team KAS. Dao's riding two straight wins, Mike S has lost two straight, and his season might hinge on an ankle in Minnesota.

Mohn's Closing Thought

Three weeks in, here's what we know: scoring points is optional, the median is the only honest judge in this league, and every week somebody new is the best team in the league for about 48 hours. Enjoy it while it lasts. It won't.

Special Shoutouts:

  • Rob, for beating his brother with half his roster in a walking boot.
  • Eric, for starting Kelce, CeeDee and McCaffrey and still losing to his brother. Tough week to share a last name.
  • Janice, for learning that 4–0 was a rental, not a purchase.
  • Mike E, for proving the fastest way to fix a season is to name your team after the guy who's going to fix it.
  • Curtis, for winning his game by 16 and still only going 1–1. Only in this league.
  • Mike S, for still owning Josh Allen, which is more than the rest of you can say.

Set those Week 4 lineups, check the injury report twice, and may your starters stay healthy, your bench stay quiet, and your opponent's quarterback need a concussion check that actually keeps him out.
