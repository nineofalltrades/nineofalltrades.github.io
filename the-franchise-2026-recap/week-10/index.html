The Franchise · Week 10 · By Mohn Jadden

Two-Thirty-Seven.

Week 10 had three upsets, two perfect lineups that lost, and one score that broke the league. Dao put up 237.26, the most points any team has ever scored in The Franchise, and Eric lost to it with the second-best number of the week. The top of the table is now a three-way tie at 13–7, and the playoff line is a three-way tie at 9–11. Four weeks left. Nobody knows anything, except Dao, who apparently knows everything.

The Headline

Dao Broke the Scoreboard

Dao scored 237.26, the highest score in Franchise history, and the record he broke was his own: 207.20, set five weeks ago. Jahmyr Gibbs went for 43.90 on 252 scrimmage yards in Munich, Joe Burrow threw for 43.76, Emmett Johnson scored 35.30 filling in for an injured Kenneth Walker, Ja’Marr Chase added 32.60 and Parker Washington chipped in 25.70. Those five scored 181.26 by themselves, more than any other team in the league scored this week. His kicker scored 3.00 and he still cleared 237.*

On the other side, Eric scored 156.44, the fifth-highest losing score in league history and the highest of his career, beating the one he set last week. He lost by 80.82, his worst regular-season loss ever, and fell into a three-way tie at 9–11 for the last playoff spot with Curtis and Team KAS. Dao has won four straight and sits a game behind the leaders. Eric did nothing wrong this week except show up on Dao’s schedule.

* Yes, the second flex. Take Blake Corum’s 22.10 out of the lineup entirely and it’s 215.16, still 13.86 clear of the best score of the one-flex era (Rob’s 201.30 in the 2020 title game). Asterisk acknowledged. Record stands.

Mohn's Closing Thought

Ten weeks in: three teams tied for first, three tied for sixth, and the guy who scored 237 is in fourth. The record book got a new top line and the standings didn’t blink. Nobody is safe, and Eric is less safe than most.
