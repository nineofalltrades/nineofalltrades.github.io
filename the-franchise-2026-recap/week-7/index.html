The Story

Tennessee Waltz

Here's how fast it turned. Through six weeks Eric had scored 730.76 points, the fewest in the league, and on Friday Yahoo had Chris beating him by 0.64. Then Tony Pollard, a running back on a Titans team that is now 1–6, carried 25 times for 129 yards and three touchdowns against Cleveland. That's the first three-rushing-touchdown game by a Titan since Derrick Henry did it in October 2021. Pollard was sitting in Eric's flex spot, projected for 15.24. He scored 38.50, the best line in the league by more than four points. Tennessee blew a 14-point lead and lost on a field goal as time expired. Eric would like everyone to know that is not his problem.

Now, about Derrick Henry. He plays for Chris. He started for Chris this week. And while Pollard was out there doing a Derrick Henry impression, the actual Derrick Henry was scoring 7.10 in a 35–13 home loss to Cincinnati, 11.61 under his projection. You can't make this up, and Mohn wouldn't dare. The rest of Eric's lineup piled on. CeeDee Lamb put up 21.90 on Monday night, and Christian McCaffrey left the first half with a thigh injury, came back, and scored 19.70. Eric did all of it with Rashee Rice and Travis Etienne on IR and Zach Charbonnet inactive. Final: 174.02 to 132.42, the biggest margin of the week by 27 points. Eric jumped five spots, from ninth to fourth, and from last in points to fourth in points. He leads this series 10–6 now, and the 41.60 is the fourth-biggest beating he's ever put on Chris. Chris didn't lose this game so much as stand next to a stampede.

📄 The Short-Term Lease, Part Two

Last week Mike S was running an Airbnb. This week the guest house belongs to first place. TJ picked up Roschon Johnson at 4:26 p.m. Wednesday and dropped him at 2:31 p.m. Saturday. One Thursday night game, watched from TJ's bench, then checkout. No security deposit returned.

Start/Sit Court: nobody's guilty this week. Not one start in the league missed by three projected points. The jury, however, has a stack of questions.

Curtis, please rise. You lost by 14.22. Keenan Allen scored 4.60 in your flex, and KC Concepcion scored 20.50 on your bench. That one swap wins the game by 1.68. Allen projected 8.84, Concepcion 8.39, so it's a coin flip and you're not guilty. But the jury notes where Allen came from: Chris dropped him at 8:04 Thursday morning for Jaylin Noel, who scored 0.70. You claimed Allen for $0 and the waiver went through at 12:34 a.m. Sunday by Yahoo's clock. Chris's castoff started in your lineup and lost you the game. Nobody won the Keenan Allen transaction, including Keenan Allen.

Chad, you too. Your best possible lineup was 128.64. You lost by 6.84 to a 128.98. Perfect wouldn't have been enough, by 0.34. But Rico Dowdle (12.50) for A.J. Brown (6.30) clears the median by 3.71 and turns an 0–2 week into 1–1. Brown projected 12.32 to Dowdle's 8.50. Right call, wrong week. Not guilty.

Dao, step forward. You won the game and missed the median by 0.95 with 64.60 on your bench. Blake Corum (18.30) for Xavier Worthy (2.60) clears it by 14.75. Worthy projected 10.22, Corum 8.80. Not guilty, and the court's aware you already took out the trash.

TJ, don't think we didn't see you. You won the game and lost to the median by 4.71 with Emanuel Wilson's 21.40 on the bench. Wilson for Coker clears it by 15.29. Wilson projected 11.31 to Coker's 12.51. Not guilty. First place doesn't get a pass, but it does get a coin flip.

Everyone else is dismissed. Janice's best possible lineup still loses by 2.94. Chris's loses by almost 40. Mike E's lineup was perfect and is covered in Hardware. Eric, Rob and Mike S went 2–0 and have nothing to answer for. Court adjourned.

Mohn's Closing Thought

Seven weeks in, this table makes about as much sense as a Titans fourth quarter. The man who was last in points a week ago is fourth. The man who's given up a thousand points is second. The man who set a perfect lineup is last. Set the best lineup you can, and then pray your opponent's running back isn't from Tennessee.
