Sunday Morning
Kelce.

Week 2 took everything we learned in Week 1, folded it into a paper airplane, and threw it straight into a ceiling fan. The Story

Sunday Morning Kelce

By Friday morning The McClusterfucks were 0–2, dead last, coming off the worst score in the league, and down more than 30 to Team KAS because Josh Allen had already scored 44.22 on Thursday night. The league had moved on. The obituary was drafted. Your commissioner, everybody.

Then Sunday at 7:32 a.m., while the rest of us were still asleep, Eric cut Bo Nix and picked up Travis Kelce. Yes, THAT Travis Kelce, just sitting on waivers. Kelce dropped 21.60. CeeDee Lamb added 32.30. Meanwhile the nine guys Mike S put around Josh Allen scored 62.70 combined, not even 20 more than Allen scored by himself. Final: 131.58–106.92. Mike S, buddy, get that man some help.

Awards

🏆 Weekly Hardware

🎁 The Regift Award

Janice cut George Kittle. Curtis paid $10 for him. Kittle scored 16 on Curtis's bench, in a loss, against Janice. Merry Christmas, Curtis. Keep the receipt.

🗑️ The Dao Memorial Tight End Fund

Dao cut Kelce in Week 1 to sign Michael Mayer. Then he cut Mayer. Then he watched Kelce drop 21.60 for Eric. Dao won anyway, so no refunds will be issued.

♻️ The Castoff Award

Eric cut Stefon Diggs in Week 1. Mike E paid $16 for him and got 19.20 back. Great buy. Still lost. That's how Mike E's season is going.

💵 The Two-Dollar Bill

TJ spent $2 on Brock Purdy, benched him, and watched him score 30.78. Now Daniels is out and Purdy's his starter. Best two bucks anybody in this league has spent.

Start/Sit Court: nobody screwed up this week. Chad's Caleb Douglas and Trevor Lawrence starts were close calls, not crimes, and he won anyway. Everything ugly in the box scores was an injury or a bad beat. Court adjourned.

Mohn's Closing Thought

Two weeks in and the lessons are simple: the free-agent pool is smarter than all of you, the median has jokes, and no lineup survives a Monday night with the Giants in it. Set your alarms. Somebody else is checking waivers at 7 a.m.

Special Shoutouts:

  • Eric, for going from the league's worst score to fourth-best in seven days.
  • Janice, for making 4–0 look like a chore.
  • Mike S, for proving one guy can't carry a whole roster. We checked.
  • Chris, for getting robbed and still sitting third.
  • Dao, for getting Ja'Marr Chase back. Now get the wins back.

Set those Week 3 lineups, check the injury report twice, and may your starters stay healthy, your bench stay quiet, and your opponent pick up the guy you just cut.
