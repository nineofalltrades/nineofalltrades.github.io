Sunday Morning
Kelce.

Week 2 took everything we learned in Week 1, folded it into a paper airplane, and threw it straight into a ceiling fan. Three favorites went down. The best player in the league lost by 25. A guy got cut, picked up off waivers before church, and won somebody a matchup. Nothing makes sense and I've never been happier.

Janice left Patrick Mahomes on her bench and won by 22 anyway, because she can. TJ's roster is basically a hospital waiting room and he's 2–2. And Mike E named his whole team after Jaxon Smith-Njigba, watched Jaxon drop 39, and still lost. Let's get into it.

The Story

Sunday Morning Kelce

By Friday morning The McClusterfucks were 0–2, dead last, coming off the worst score in the league, and down more than 30 to Team KAS because Josh Allen had already scored 44.22 on Thursday night. The league had moved on. The obituary was drafted. Your commissioner, everybody.

Then Sunday at 7:32 a.m., while the rest of us were still asleep, Eric cut Bo Nix and picked up Travis Kelce. Yes, THAT Travis Kelce, the one Dao cut in Week 1 so he could sign Michael Mayer, who Dao has also since cut. Kelce dropped 21.60. CeeDee Lamb added 32.30. Meanwhile the nine guys Mike S surrounded Josh Allen with scored 62.70 combined, not even 20 more than Allen scored by himself. Final: 131.58–106.92. Eric went from dead last to the biggest win of the week, and Dao got to watch his own trash walk into somebody else's end zone. Mike S, buddy. Allen can't also play receiver.

Mohn's Closing Thought

Two weeks in and we've already learned the only rule in this league: the free-agent pool is smarter than all of you. Kelce was sitting there. Diggs was sitting there. Purdy cost two dollars. Meanwhile Janice is 4–0 with Mahomes on her bench, and the rest of us are refreshing the injury report like it owes us money.

Special Shoutouts:

  • Eric, for turning a Sunday-morning waiver claim into a 25-point win. Set an alarm next week too.
  • Janice, for benching 35 points and winning by 22. Show-off.
  • TJ, for going 2–0 in a week where half his roster was in a sling.
  • Rob, for scoring enough to win again and losing again. Hang in there.
  • Dao, for winning the matchup and losing the median by the width of a fingernail.

Set those Week 3 lineups, check the injury report twice, and may your starters stay healthy, your bench stay quiet, and your opponent pick up the guy you just cut.
