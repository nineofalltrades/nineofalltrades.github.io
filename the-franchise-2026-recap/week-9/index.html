The Franchise · Week 9 · By Mohn Jadden

Eighty-Three Points.
Zero Wins.

Week 9 had two quarterbacks score more than 41 fantasy points, and both of them lost. Stroud put up 41.66 for Eric and lost by 1.32, and Love put up 41.58 for Mike E and lost by 8.38. TJ and Rob are tied at 13–5 at the top. A quarterback can’t win a week alone, and two of them just proved it at once.

The Headline

Eighty-Three Points, Zero Wins

Take a quarterback who scores 41 and hand him to a team. Stroud did it for Eric, 41.66, and Eric lost by 1.32 to Curtis. Love did it for Mike E, 41.58, and Mike E lost by 8.38 to Rob. That is 83.24 points from the position that is supposed to win you the week, and a combined record of 0–2. The difference was everything else: Eric got 94.66 from Stroud, Lamb and McCaffrey and 48.40 from his other seven starters, and Mike E got 70.58 from Love, Fairbairn and the Packers defense and 50.90 from his other seven.

Eric’s 143.06 is the highest score he has ever lost with, by 0.02, and the 19th-highest losing score in league history. Curtis climbed from ninth to seventh on it, and his hero, Puka Nacua, went for an MRI Monday: sprained MCL and PCL plus turf toe, at least a month, likely injured reserve, no torn ACL. At the top, TJ beat Chris by 31.60 and Rob beat Mike E by 8.38, so both are 13–5, with TJ first on the tiebreaker. The median decided the rest, and it had a very specific week.
