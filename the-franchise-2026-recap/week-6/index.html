The Story

Contempt of Court

Here's the case file. Drake Maye has now sat behind Trevor Lawrence on Chad's bench five straight weeks, and last week he did it while throwing four touchdowns and scoring 45.76, the best line in the league. The court looked at the projections and found Maye ahead by 2.57, just under the three-point bar, and let Chad walk. This week Yahoo had Maye at 23.12 and Lawrence at 20.11. That's 3.01. For the first time all year the gavel was in the air. And Chad set his alarm for a 9:30 a.m. kickoff in London and started Lawrence anyway. Lawrence went 24 of 41 for 306 yards and four touchdowns: 34.04. Maye threw three touchdowns against the Jets and scored 26.00, which is a perfectly lovely afternoon that came in eight points short. Chad also got a 60-yard field goal from Cam Little to tie the game with about two minutes left. Jacksonville still lost, on a walk-off from Houston's kicker. Chad didn't care. Chad wasn't playing for Jacksonville. Chad was playing for spite.

The rest of the lineup piled on. James Cook put up 25.00, Chuba Hubbard came back from the bye with 22.40, Jonathan Taylor added 22.20, and the Steelers defense chipped in 10. On the other side, Chris was running a field hospital. Goff and Tee Higgins on bye, Montgomery and Judkins out, Terry McLaurin carted off in the first quarter, and Derrick Henry fumbling in Cleveland on his way to 6.20. Final: 161.14 to 99.36. That's the eighth-highest score Chad has put up in 195 games in this league. A week ago Chad was dead last in points scored. Now he's in second place. He leads this series 16–8 and has won the last three meetings by 58.90, 22.84 and 61.78. Chris isn't in a rivalry with Chad. He's in a hostage situation. The court would like the record to show it was right on paper. Chad would like the record to show the scoreboard.

Start/Sit Court: four straight weeks without a conviction, and then two in one Sunday.

Everyone else is dismissed. Mike E needed two swaps, already covered under Bench Goat. Janice, Chris and Dao couldn't have won if they'd started every player on their roster, and Janice's inactive receiver had no eligible backup left to play. Rob, as usual, had nothing to answer for. Mohn's Closing Thought

For one week, The Franchise did exactly what it was told. Every favorite won. Every projection came true, except the one Chad ignored, and he's the one who had the best Sunday in the league. Follow the numbers and you'll be fine. Ignore them and you'd better be Chad.
